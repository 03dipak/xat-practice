# COVERAGE — what is trained, what is written, what is pending

**This project trains 28 of the 95 XAT questions: the QA&DI section only.** Part 1
is 75 questions across three sections — QA&DI 28, VA&LR 26, DM 21 — and this
project builds nothing for VA&LR or DM. GK (20, Part 2) is excluded from the
percentile by XLRI and is out of scope. Read every number below as a number about
**one section**, not about the exam.

Of that 28-question section, **1 of 40 trained subtopics is written**.

## How this file stays true

The block below is generated by `xat-practice coverage`, which is a pure function
over `syllabus.py` and `registry.py`. `tests/test_cli.py` extracts the fenced block
and compares it to that function, so **code moves and this document does not, the
suite fails**. Nothing here is maintained by hand.

That is the design, not a preference. MEASURED 2026-10-02:
`xat-practice weightage` printed `written: 4` — the **item** count — on a line
whose neighbours were **subtopic** counts, on a project with 40 subtopics. It read
as "four topics done" when the truth was one. A status file that people edit would
carry that lie for longer, and nobody would know.

## What the numbers mean, and what they do not

**`q/yr` is a TOPIC figure and is deliberately absent from the table.** The weight
model is per topic, measured over 196 questions (28 per paper × 7 papers, 2020–2026)
from a coaching compilation — XLRI publishes no breakdown. No source gives a
per-subtopic frequency, so putting a topic's rate on a subtopic row would be
inventing one.

**`traps` is the real capacity number.** It is what bounds how many *different*
wrong moves a subtopic can drill, and D9's rule is that a trap we cannot name is a
trap we cannot set.

**`block capacity` is a target, not a gate.** A 20-item block wants one headline
trap per item with none repeated (`BLOCK_TRAPS_FULL = 20`). At least
`BLOCK_SHAPES_RELAXED = 10` is what the *proposed* `G17` relaxation would need —
**and that relaxation is not built.** `G17` still demands all-distinct shapes
within a set, which no 20-item set can satisfy on one subtopic. See D19.

## Second-hand weightages, and why they are not merged

Two external summaries were supplied by the owner on 2026-10-02, both produced by
assistants and **both citing the same two URLs** (cracku.in and careers360.com).
That is **one source counted twice**, not two sources agreeing. They are recorded
here as provenance-tagged secondary claims and are **never merged into
`syllabus.TOPICS`**, which is measured-from-secondary-source of the same family.

| Geometry & Mensuration ~3–6 | **4.57 q/yr** | **agrees** |

| Number System ~2–3 | **2.86 q/yr** | **agrees**, closely |
| Modern Math low, "do basics" | `prob_comb` 1.00, `venn` 0.57, `puzzle` 1.29 | **agrees** — all P3 |
| Trigonometry 0–1 question | **excluded**, reason recorded as "0–1 per year" | **they confirm our exclusion** |
| both place DI **late**, after geometry and algebra | D14 puts Geometry first | **independent support for the owner's call** |

**Where they are wrong, or incomplete.** Both say "check the latest brochure,
the count changes by year" — correct, and the reason this project pins XLRI's own
2026 notification rather than a coaching page. Neither gives a denominator: their
percentages are ranges (`18–35%` of a section), and **a range of 5–7 questions is
an estimate, not a measurement.** Ours closes to exactly 28 per year or
`self_check()` raises.

Neither source was used to add, remove or reweight a topic. D4's additions were
made on the measured table, and the owner's own list stands as given.
